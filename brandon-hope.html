PROJECT DETAILS

Brandon & Hope

Client wedding website

HTML · CSS
Responsive layout
Google Forms RSVP

Everything guests need.
One thoughtful place.

A wedding website needs to feel personal while keeping practical information easy to find. This project brings together the couple’s story, venue details, schedule, travel guidance, and an RSVP handoff.

A clear path through the day

The page organizes information into focused sections, helping guests move from the story to the logistics. A warm palette and expressive typography reflect the celebratory character of the event.

A practical RSVP approach

Prominent RSVP links direct guests to Google Forms, giving the couple a familiar way to manage responses. The public site remains a straightforward, accessible source of event information.

Designed for different screens

Flexible grids and responsive typography adapt the content for desktop and mobile, including stacked details and full-width actions on smaller screens.
